Privacy Policy

Bloomland Color Adventure, by Gamebloom
Effective date: 11 November 2026

In short: Bloomland Color Adventure is a coloring and letter-learning game for young children. We do not collect, store or share any personal information. The game has no ads, no accounts and no analytics.

Who we are

Gamebloom is an independent developer. Bloomland Color Adventure ("the game") is our app for Android. If you have any question about this policy, write to mohammed_alii93@hotmail.com.

Information we collect

None. We do not collect personal information from children or from parents. In particular, the game does not:

  • ask anyone to create an account or sign in;
  • show advertising or use an advertising identifier;
  • use analytics or tracking tools;
  • ask for location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone;
  • contain chat, social features or links that take a child out of the game without a grown-up.

Information that stays on the device

To make the game work, some things are saved on the device only and are never sent to us or to anyone else:

  • the first name or nickname a child may type so the characters can greet them (this is optional);
  • game progress, such as stars, stickers and unlocked letters;
  • the pictures the child colors or draws;
  • settings, such as language and sound.

Uninstalling the game, or clearing its data in the device settings, deletes all of this.

Purchases

The game is free to download and includes one optional, one-time purchase that unlocks all letters. The purchase button is behind a parental gate. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play; we never see or store card details or other payment information. Google's handling of purchase data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Internet access

The game works offline. It uses the internet only to talk to Google Play about the optional purchase (to show the price, complete a purchase or restore one).

Children's privacy

The game is designed for children and is built to follow the Google Play Families Policy, the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Because we do not collect personal information, there is nothing for us to share, sell or use for advertising.

Parents' choices

Settings and the purchase screen are protected by a question meant for grown-ups. Parents can delete everything the game has saved by uninstalling it. If you believe the game has collected information about your child, contact us at the address above and we will look into it promptly.

Changes to this policy

If the game changes in a way that affects privacy, we will update this page and its effective date before the change reaches players.
